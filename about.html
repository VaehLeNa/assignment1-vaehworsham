About the Black Student Union

Who We Are

The Black Student Union (BSU) at Mount St. Joseph University is a student-led organization that celebrates culture, encourages collaboration, and creates opportunities for students to make meaningful contributions to campus life. Through shared experiences and student involvement, BSU works to create an organization where different voices, perspectives, and ideas are valued.

Our Mission

BSU is committed to celebrating culture, encouraging student leadership, and creating opportunities for connection and positive change. We aim to make a lasting impact through our programming, partnerships, and involvement both on campus and within the surrounding community.

Our Goals

Community

Strengthen relationships among students by creating opportunities for collaboration, shared experiences, and meaningful campus connections.

Leadership

Empower students to take initiative, contribute ideas, build confidence, and develop the skills needed to become active leaders within their organization and community.

Service

Create opportunities to support others through volunteer efforts, community partnerships, and initiatives that encourage students to make a difference beyond campus.

Executive Board & Leadership

The BSU Executive Board works together to lead the organization, support its members, coordinate initiatives, and create meaningful experiences throughout the academic year. Each position has an important role in keeping the organization organized, active, and connected.

President

Provides overall leadership and direction for BSU, represents the organization, leads executive board meetings, and helps ensure the organization's mission and goals are carried out.

Vice President

Supports the President, helps oversee executive board operations, coordinates organizational initiatives, and assists with communication, delegation, and planning.

Secretary

Maintains meeting minutes, attendance, records, and important organizational documents. Helps communicate announcements and keep board information organized.

Treasurer

Oversees financial records, monitors budgets and expenses, tracks organizational spending, and helps ensure responsible management of BSU funds.

Social Media Coordinator

Manages BSU's social media platforms, creates promotional content, shares announcements, and highlights events and organizational activities.

Event Coordinator(s)

Develops, plans, and coordinates BSU events and programs. Works with the executive board to organize logistics, communicate event details, manage preparations, and create engaging experiences for students.
