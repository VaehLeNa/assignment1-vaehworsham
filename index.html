Welcome to BSU

Culture. Connection. Community.

Welcome to the Black Student Union at Mount St. Joseph University! BSU brings students together through shared experiences, cultural appreciation, meaningful connections, and opportunities to make a difference. We are a space to connect, grow, celebrate, and belong.

Learn More About Us

Our Purpose

We strive to cultivate an inclusive campus environment where students feel seen, supported, and valued. Through engaging events, cultural experiences, campus involvement, and service, BSU creates opportunities for students to learn from one another and strengthen the Mount community.

Get Involved

There's a place for you here.

Whether you're interested in attending events, sharing ideas, building friendships, or stepping into leadership, BSU welcomes you. You don't have to be a member of a particular background to participate. Bring your perspective, your energy, and your willingness to connect.
